All three major indexes ended the week at record highs, and the S&P has now risen nine consecutive weeks, the longest winning streak since 2023. So far, investors are still buying. The tech-heavy NASDAQ led the way, rising just under 2.4% for the week. The S&P 500 grew 1.4% and the Dow increased 0.9%. The AI boom minted two new trillion-dollar companies this week. Shares in memory chip makers Micron and SK Hynix surged nearly 30% and 20%, respectively, to cross the trillion-dollar valuation mark. They're the latest additions after Samsung joined one of the stock market's most exclusive clubs earlier this month.
到目前为止，投资者仍在买入。以科技股为主的纳斯达克指数领涨，本周涨幅接近2.4%。标普500指数上涨1.4%，道琼斯工业平均指数上涨0.9%。人工智能热潮本周又催生了两家万亿美元市值公司。存储芯片制造商美光科技和 SK Hynix 的股价分别飙升近30%和20%，市值双双突破万亿美元大关。本月早些时候，三星也加入了股市中这个最顶级的俱乐部，而它们则是最新加入者。

For years, memory chips were seen as a commodity business, the tech equivalent of selling gasoline, but investors are now trading chips as one of the most precious resources in the AI economy. In fact, the combined value of Samsung, SK Hynix, and Micron now exceeds the market value of the world's three largest oil companies. Every AI model needs enormous amounts of memory to store and move data, creating a global shortage of semiconductors. According to data provider Ornn, spot prices for a common AI memory chip known as DDR5 have more than doubled since November. Stock prices have mirrored those gains. Micron shares have tripled since the end of March, helping it become the fastest company to ever go from a $500 billion valuation to a trillion dollar one. As memory chips become the hottest commodity, oil stocks are getting the cold shoulder.
多年来，存储芯片一直被视为一种大宗商品业务，相当于科技行业里的“卖汽油”。但现在，投资者正把芯片当作人工智能经济中最宝贵的资源之一来交易。事实上，三星、SK Hynix 和美光的总市值现在已经超过全球三大石油公司的市值。每一个人工智能模型都需要海量存储空间来保存和传输数据，这造成了全球半导体短缺。数据提供商 Ornn 称，一种常见的人工智能存储芯片 DDR5 的现货价格自去年11月以来已经翻了一倍多。股价也反映了这些涨幅。美光的股价自3月底以来已经涨至原来的三倍，帮助其成为有史以来从5000亿美元市值升至1万亿美元市值速度最快的公司。随着存储芯片成为最热门的大宗商品，石油股却正在受到冷落。

Energy was the worst-performing sector in the S&P 500 this week, as crude prices saw their biggest monthly decline since the onset of the pandemic. Reports that Washington and Tehran may be inching toward a possible agreement that would reopen the Strait of Hormuz have traders unwinding bets that the conflict could lead to a prolonged supply shock. Investors have gotten used to whipsawing headlines as tensions in the Middle East drag on, but they still have some impact. The S&P Energy Index fell more than 5% over the week as Brent Crude slid 11% to $92.05 a barrel. But don't cry for oil investors just yet. The conflict in the Middle East sent energy stocks soaring earlier this year, and the S&P Energy Index remains up more than 25% year to date. Ford was one of the week's biggest winners as investor's enthusiasm for a new subsidiary continued to rev up. 
本周，能源板块是标普500指数中表现最差的板块，原因是原油价格出现了自疫情暴发以来最大的月度跌幅。有报道称，华盛顿和德黑兰可能正逐步接近一项潜在协议，该协议将重新开放霍尔木兹海峡；这使交易员开始解除此前押注冲突会导致长期供应冲击的头寸。随着中东紧张局势持续，投资者已经习惯了反复摇摆的新闻标题，但这些消息仍然会产生影响。布伦特原油价格下跌11%，至每桶92.05美元，标普能源指数本周下跌超过5%。不过，现在还不必为石油投资者感到难过。今年早些时候，中东冲突曾推动能源股大幅上涨，标普能源指数今年以来仍上涨超过25%。福特是本周表现最好的股票之一，因为投资者对其新子公司的热情继续升温。

Shares in the automaker continued to climb nearly 17% this week, following the company's announcement of an energy storage division called Ford Energy earlier this month. After pulling back from the electric vehicle market, Ford is repurposing batteries originally intended for EVs into stationary energy storage systems for AI data centers, power utilities, and industrial customers. One Morgan Stanley analyst recently estimated that the business could eventually be worth $10 billion on its own. Investors see the move as a way for Ford to cash in on the AI frenzy, and that's right at a moment when the auto industry is slogging through an EV slowdown and broader downturn. Automakers are increasingly warning that new car sales could shrink this year. Roughly, 1 million potential buyers have left the new car market since the start of the decade, and executives don't expect them back anytime soon as consumers grapple with high interest rates, stubborn inflation, and elevated gas prices.
